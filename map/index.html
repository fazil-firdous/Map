What the comparison shows

  • Only 590 OSM places match an Overture place (559 distinct Overture places, since some match twice). That is 24% of named OSM places and 6% of Overture businesses.
  • OSM adds 1,983 places Overture lacks, mostly clinics, petrol pumps, workshops, restaurants and downtown shops.
  • Overture has phone numbers for 78% of its businesses. OSM has them for 2%.
  • From the overlap, a capture-recapture estimate puts the city's mappable businesses at 40,000 or more. Together, the two sources hold about 11,600.
