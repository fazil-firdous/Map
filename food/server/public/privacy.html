Kezia's Food: Privacy policy

Kezia's Food lets you order food from restaurants in Kulgam district. Restaurants prepare and deliver your order with their own riders. This page explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Your phone number and name, to log you in with a one-time code and so the restaurant can call you about your order.
  • Delivery addresses: the pin you drop on the map and your landmark.
  • Your orders: items, prices, payment method, the UPI reference number you enter, ratings and reviews.
  • Rider location: if you deliver for a restaurant, your phone's location while an order is out for delivery, so the customer can see it arrive. It is not collected at other times.

Who sees it

  • The restaurant you order from sees your name, phone number, address and order.
  • You see the rider's name, phone and live location while your order is on its way.
  • An SMS provider delivers login codes. Our hosting provider stores the data on our server. We don't sell data or show ads.

Payments

You pay the restaurant directly by UPI, using your own UPI app, or in cash. Kezia's Food never receives or stores your bank or UPI PIN details.

Delete your data

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. This removes your account, addresses and login. Past orders stay in the restaurant's records without your name or number. You can also email fazil.mastu@gmail.com.
