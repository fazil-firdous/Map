Kezia's Food: Terms, cancellations and refunds

Kezia's Food connects you with restaurants in Kulgam district. The restaurant prepares your food, delivers it with its own rider and is responsible for its quality, prices and taxes.

Ordering and paying

  • The restaurant must accept your order within a few minutes, or it is cancelled automatically. You pay nothing before that.
  • For UPI orders you pay the restaurant directly, after it accepts. The restaurant confirms it received the money before it starts cooking.
  • Cash on delivery is available where the restaurant allows it. Refusing cash orders at the door can disable cash on delivery for your account.

Cancelling

  • Free before you pay, and for a short time after the restaurant confirms your payment (shown in the app, at most 5 minutes).
  • After that the kitchen starts cooking and the order can't be cancelled in the app. Call the restaurant if there's a problem.

Refunds

  • If a paid order is cancelled (by you within the free window, or by the restaurant at any time), the restaurant refunds the full amount to the UPI account you paid from, normally within 24 hours.
  • The app shows the refund's status. If it doesn't arrive, contact us from the app and we will follow it up with the restaurant.
