About this app

Kezia's Food is Kulgam's own food app. Order from the restaurants you love, pay them directly by UPI or cash, and watch your food come to your door on the live map. Every restaurant delivers with its own rider, so your food comes straight from the kitchen.

🍕Kulgam's best kitchensPizza, wazwan, tandoori, Chinese, shakes and more.
📲Pay the restaurant directlyScan the UPI QR or tap GPay, PhonePe or Paytm. Or pay cash on delivery.
🛵Watch your rider liveSee your food on the map from the kitchen to your door.
🎁Free delivery on your first orderNew customers get their first delivery free.
🧑‍🍳For restaurants tooOne app to take orders, manage the menu, riders and earnings.
