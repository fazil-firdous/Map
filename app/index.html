How the app looks and works

This prototype runs on the real merged dataset. Everything you tap is live: search, browse by type and area, open a shop, see it on the map. The add and edit forms show the flow but save nothing yet.

Three screens do most of the work

  • Explore starts from what people ask for in Srinagar: a type of shop in an area. Kashmiri crafts get their own row, because visitors look for them first.
  • Shop page puts Call, WhatsApp, Directions and Instagram at thumb height. Most shops here sell over WhatsApp and Instagram, so these matter more than a website.
  • Map shows the same results you searched, not every pin in the city.

Trust is shown, not hidden

  • Each shop shows how many sources list it and a trust bar, so people can tell a confirmed shop from a guess.
  • An "Approximate location" tag appears when we only know the neighbourhood, which is about 1 shop in 10.
  • Nothing is marked "Verified" until a person confirms it, either the owner or a visitor who was there.

People fill the gaps

  • Add a shop takes the name, type, area, phone or Instagram, and a pin. It needs five fields and no account.
  • Suggest an edit and Mark as closed on every shop page keep the data fresh.
  • Own this shop? lets an owner claim the listing with an OTP to the shop's phone, then update hours, photos and their Instagram.
  • New entries go to a review queue first. Two independent confirmations, or one owner claim, make a shop "Verified".
