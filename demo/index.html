Srinagar Bazaar

A working demo on 10,055 real businesses and 150 sights. Every screen is live, so tap anything. Searches understand plain requests and always show the nearest places first.

  1. Search the way you talk. "I want to buy bread", "need dawai", "kong", "visit Mughal gardens". Suggestions appear as you type.
  2. Open a shop. Call, WhatsApp, directions, save and share are one tap away. "How sure are we" shows how well each listing is confirmed.
  3. Map tab. Pins and swipeable cards stay in sync.
  4. Ask tab. Post a need, like "who has a 32-inch TV in stock?", and nearby shops reply on WhatsApp.
  5. Saved tab. Bookmarks stay on this device.
