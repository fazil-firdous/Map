Privacy policy

Srinagar Bazaar · Last updated 16 October 2026

Srinagar Bazaar ("we") is an app for finding shops, services and places in Srinagar. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • A random app ID. Created on first launch so the app can save your contributions. It is not linked to your name unless you add one.
  • Your name, only if you type one (shown on the Champions leaderboard).
  • Your mobile number, only if you verify it, to claim a shop, receive Ask replies, or receive prizes. We never show it publicly. Shop owners' business numbers are shown on their shop page because that is the purpose of a listing.
  • Location. Used on your phone to sort shops by distance. It is sent to us only when you add a shop, confirm a shop, or send an Ask request, and then only the point needed for that action.
  • Things you send us: new shops, edits, closure reports, photos, Ask requests and replies.
  • Anonymous counts of shop page views and taps on Call, WhatsApp, Directions and Share. These are counted per shop per day, without your ID, and shown only to that shop's verified owner.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't show ads based on your personal data.
  • We don't read your contacts, messages or files. Messages you send on WhatsApp go through your own WhatsApp; we never see them.

Who we share it with

  • Shops you send an Ask request to see the request text and roughly how far away you are. Your number is shared only if you choose to call or message them.
  • An SMS provider in India sends verification codes to your number.
  • Our hosting provider stores the data on our server.

How long we keep it

Ask requests expire after 48 hours and are deleted after 90 days. Contributions to the shop directory (new shops, edits, photos) stay as long as they are useful. Daily view counts are kept for up to 2 years.

Your choices

You can use the app without verifying a phone number or sharing location. To see or delete your data, email fazil.mastu@gmail.com from any address and include your app ID (shown in Me → About) or verified number. We reply within 30 days.

Delete your data

In the app: Me → About & privacy → Delete my data. This removes your account, verified number, requests and points straight away. Shops you added stay in the directory without your name. Photos you uploaded are removed on request.

Without the app: email fazil.mastu@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your verified number. We delete within 7 days and confirm by email.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Data sources

Shop listings combine open data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; Overture Maps Foundation; AllThePlaces) with information added by the people of Srinagar and by shop owners.
